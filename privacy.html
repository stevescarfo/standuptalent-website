Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Stand Up Talent is an app for comedians, agents and comedy fans. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the app and this website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored only in encrypted form), account type (comedian, agent or fan), name or stage name, @username, and the home base city and state you choose to show.
  • Profile content: your photo, bio, photos you upload, links to videos, audio and social media accounts, and recommendations you write or receive.
  • Private home address (optional): comedians can add a street address. Only you and our admins can see it; we will use it for features such as travel distances to shows.
  • Agent details: your agency name. Admins check agents are real before they can send offers.
  • Bookings and availability: shows in My Book (venue, date, time, set length, role, ticket link), with private notes and pay that only you see, plus days you mark available or blocked and availability lists you send to agents.
  • Messages and offers: messages you send and receive, message requests, and offers and inquiries between agents and comedians.
  • Activity: who you follow, favorite clubs and comedians, alert settings, rosters, and reports and blocks you make.
  • Extra contact details (optional): other email addresses and phone numbers you add in Settings.
  • Support: anything you send us when you contact support.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: if you allow it, the app uses your phone's location to show clubs and open mics near you. It is sent to our servers to find nearby venues and is not stored with your account. You can turn it off in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Notifications: if you allow push notifications, we store a token for your phone so we can send alerts.
  • Profile views: when a signed-in person views a comedian's profile, we count the view (unless the viewer has Private Viewing Mode on).
  • Purchases: if you buy a plan, the App Store or Google Play processes the payment. We never see your card details. We receive the plan, its renewal or end date and the store it came from.
  • Ads: free accounts see ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your phone's advertising identifier to show and measure ads, but only after you give permission where the law requires it (for example Apple's tracking prompt on iPhone, or Google's consent form in some regions). Without that permission, ads are not personalized.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers keep standard logs (such as IP address, device type and error reports) to keep the service running and secure.

How we use information

  • To run the app: show profiles, schedules and venues, deliver messages, offers and alerts, and keep your account signed in.
  • To keep people safe: review reports, verify agents, remove content that breaks our rules, and suspend or ban accounts when needed.
  • To provide plans you buy and show ads on free accounts.
  • To send account emails, such as sign-up codes and password resets.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers to run Stand Up Talent. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage (hosted in the United States).
  • Expo: delivering push notifications.
  • Resend: sending account emails.
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google: processing and managing plan purchases.
  • Google AdMob: showing ads to free accounts.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect the safety of our users or the public, or as part of a sale or merger of the business (in which case this policy would continue to apply).

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is open. When you close your account in Settings, your account and the content you created (profile, photos, bookings, messages you sent and the rest) are deleted straight away. Copies in our backups are removed within 30 days. Messages you sent to someone else are deleted with your account. We may keep records we're required to keep by law, and our admin log of safety actions.

Your choices

  • See and change your information: edit your profile, home base, address and settings in the app.
  • Privacy settings: Private Viewing Mode, who can follow you, read receipts, whether people can find you by email or phone, and whether to share your availability with agents.
  • Notifications and email: turn push alerts on or off and choose how often you get emails.
  • Location and tracking: manage location access and ad tracking in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Close Account deletes your account and data.
  • Ask us: email us to access, correct or delete your information. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have further rights; we will honor them.

Children

Stand Up Talent is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use encryption in transit, encrypted password storage, and access rules that limit each person to the information they're allowed to see. No service can be perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
