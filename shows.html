Festivals, contests and showcases

The Festivals board lists comedy festivals, contests and showcases taking submissions across the country, with the deadline, the entry fee and how to apply. It's for members, and joining is free.

  • Save a festival and get a reminder a week before it closes, and the day before.
  • Apply in a few taps: email festivals get your submission ready to send, and the rest come with a kit to copy your bio, credits and clips from.
  • Track every submission in Applied, and add accepted dates to My Book.
  • Agents and producers can post their own festival and take submissions in the app.
