Plans

Free to join, free to book. Plans are an upgrade for people who want more, never a gate to getting started.

For comedians

Comedian Pro

$4.99 a month

  • Pro comics show up first when bookers search Talent, with a PRO badge
  • Fan insights: weekly growth, fans near your shows and the clubs they follow
  • Income, miles and a tax spreadsheet for any dates, not just the past month
  • Plan bookings and send avails any date ahead
  • 30 photos and 20 videos on your profile
  • No ads

Free: your profile, My Book a month ahead, offers, messages, avails to agents, Open Mic Findr, open mic requests and the Festivals board.

For agents and producers

Agent Pro

$4.99 a month

  • List shows and open mics any date ahead
  • Post open calls that comics apply to
  • Fan draw: see fans near the rooms you book
  • Team logins for up to 5 people
  • A PRO badge on your agent page

Free: offers, rosters, avails, lineups, call sheets, Run the show and payments, with shows up to a month ahead.

For clubs and venues

Venue Pro

$9.99 a month

  • Everything in Agent Pro
  • A Featured badge and the top of Clubs
  • “Booked by” you on your venue's page

For a club or venue with its own room, verified in My venues. Booking lots of different rooms? Agent Pro is the plan for you.

Perform and book?

Pro Duo

$7.99 a month, instead of $9.98 for both

  • Comedian Pro on your talent side
  • Agent Pro on your booking side
  • One account: switch sides at the top of your profile

For fans

Ad-free

$1.99 a month

  • Browse comedians, clubs and shows with no ads
  • Support the comedy scene

Free: follow comedians and clubs, show alerts, Shows near you tonight, the Clubs map and Open Mic Findr.

Prices are in US dollars. Plans renew monthly through the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel any time there.
