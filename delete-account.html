Delete your Stand Up Talent account

Stand Up Talent, by Stand Up Talent. Last updated November 18, 2026

You can delete your account and data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Stand Up Talent and log in.
  2. Tap Profile, then the gear at the top to open Settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Close Account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. This can't be undone.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email support@standuptalent.com from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete my account and your username. We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details and profile (name, username, bio, home base and private address, contact details, links).
  • Your photos, videos, credits, recommendations, availability, bookings and payment records.
  • Messages you sent, offers, follows, saved items and notification settings.

Copies in our backups are removed within 30 days.

What we keep

  • Records we're required to keep by law.
  • Our admin log of safety actions (for example, a ban for harassment), so a banned person can't simply start again.

Paid plans

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings too.

Delete some data, keep your account

You can delete individual photos, videos, recommendations, messages and bookings in the app at any time without closing your account.
