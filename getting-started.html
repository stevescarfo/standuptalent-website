Getting Started

The comedy scene in one app.

Stand Up Talent puts comedians, agents and producers, clubs and fans in one place. It's free to join and free to book.

  • Comedians: a profile that gets you booked, My Book for your calendar, avails to every agent in two taps, Mic Findr, and getting paid.
  • Agents and producers: build lineups, run show night with call sheets and a live timer, pay the lineup, and post open calls.
  • Clubs and venues: get your room listed and kept current, run your own open mic, and get Featured with Venue Pro.
  • Fans: follow comics and clubs, get show alerts, and find comedy near you.
