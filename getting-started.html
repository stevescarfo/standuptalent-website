Getting Started

About a minute each, with captions and a voiceover.

  • Getting started 1:12

    Sign up, pick your account type, set up your profile, find comedians and clubs, add favorites and send a message.

  • My Book for comedians 1:06

    Keep your calendar up to date, add shows, send your avails to agents in the app or anywhere, and handle offers.

    Log pay, miles and travel costs for every show, open mics too, see your totals and export a CSV for tax time.

  • Booking tools for agents 1:01

    My bookings and offers, rosters, finding a comic’s open dates, sending offers and your avails inbox.
