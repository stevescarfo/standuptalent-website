Getting Started

The comedy scene in one app.

Stand Up Talent puts comedians, agents and producers, clubs and fans in one place. It's free to join and free to book.

  • Comedians: a profile that gets you booked, My Book for your calendar, avails to every agent in two taps, Open Mic Findr, the Festivals board, Promote for every show, and Payments that track what you’re owed.
  • Agents and producers: build lineups, run show night with call sheets and a live timer, set when you pay and pay the lineup, and post open calls.
  • Clubs and venues: get your room listed and kept current, run your own open mic, and get Featured with Venue Pro.
  • Fans: follow comics and clubs, get show alerts, and see what’s on near you tonight in Shows.
